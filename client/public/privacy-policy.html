grails.de Micro E-Racer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Micro E-Racer handles information when it is played: in a web browser at micro.grails.de, in the iPhone and iPad app, and in the Apple Watch app.

Overview

The game is designed to minimize data use. It does not require account creation, does not maintain user profiles, and does not collect or store personal data for analytics, marketing, or account management.

Information Handling

The game does not ask for email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials, and it does not create user accounts.

The only thing a player types in is the name they race under. It can be any nickname of up to 16 characters and does not need to be a real name.

The game does not use cookies, analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavior tracking technologies.

The game loads nothing from third parties: its font, sounds, and voices come from the game's own server or app, and everything else is generated on the device.

On Your Device

So that they are there next time, the game remembers the name you race under, your picked car and track, the sound setting, and your touch control settings on your device: in the browser's local storage, or in the app's settings.

These stay on the device. Clearing the site's data in the browser, or deleting the app, removes them.

Online Races

Pressing Play joins a race on the game server at micro.grails.de, where players and computer-driven cars race together without an account.

To make the race work, the game sends the server the name you race under, your picked car and track, and while you race your car's position and speed and the items you use. The server shares your name, car, and race events with the other players in the same race.

This data is used only while you are in the race.

It is not stored after you leave the race, and the server does not log it.

No persistent player profile, friend list, chat, match history, or high-score list is created.

Like any web server, the game server receives your device's IP address as part of the connection. It is used only to carry the connection and is not logged or stored.

Offline Races and Apple Watch

When the server cannot be reached, the race runs entirely on the device and nothing is sent.

The Apple Watch app always races offline and sends nothing.

Third-Party Services

The game does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

The game server and the connection to it are used only to pass the data needed for live races, not to create persistent user records.

Children’s Privacy

Because the game does not collect or store personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the app developer through the contact method provided on the app’s App Store product page.

App Store Privacy Summary

For App Store privacy labeling purposes, this app is intended to operate without collecting data linked to the user and without tracking users across apps or websites. Temporary race data, including the name a player races under, is used only to provide app functionality during an active race and is not stored after the race ends.
