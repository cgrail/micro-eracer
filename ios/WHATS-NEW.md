# What's New

The App Store's "What's New in This Version" text, one section per released version. Plain ASCII only: App Store Connect
rejects bullets, emoji and typographic dashes or quotes in that field.

## 1.0.1

- Race each other, not just the Bots: Play now joins the Race that is already running, whatever Track you had up. The start screen shows that Race's Track and who is in it, and Join the Race takes you there. When none is running, you pick the Track and everyone after you races on it.
- New and changed Tracks now come straight from the server, no app update needed.
- When the online game has moved on, the app tells you and takes you to the update. Until then you can still race the Bots offline.
- A livelier start screen: comic rays, chequered flags and bobbing stickers, with the Body and the Track each between their arrows.
- New Players start with a racing name like Joe Ram or Max Volt.
- A new app icon.
- Tap the i on the start screen to see who made the game, how and why, and which build you are running.
- Apple Watch: the Digital Crown is now a steering wheel with notches that stays where you leave it, with two notches for straight on, and your E-Car lines itself up with the road while you steer straight. You can also steer by touching the left or right half of the screen. The new action button shows your Item and uses it, and an E-Car stuck against a wall is put back on the road by itself.

## 1.0

- Micro E-Racer on iPhone and iPad: tiny electric cars, big chaos, now as an app.
- Race against up to 7 others in the same Races as the browser game, or against the Bots when you are offline.
- Your E-Car accelerates by itself: hold left or right anywhere on the screen to steer, and tap the button above the steering pad to use your Item.
- Ram, Zap and Drift round a kitchen, a garden, a desk and a bathtub, and keep an eye on your battery.
- On Apple Watch: race the Bots, steer with the Digital Crown and tap to use your Item.
